How to read the map

Methodology

Electricity demand means electricity generation adjusted for net imports and exports. It is not the same as domestic electricity generation and does not cover all energy used for transport or heating.

In TWh mode, colors use the same logarithmic scale every year so countries with very different demand levels remain visible. In Δ vs 2019 mode, they show percentage change from the baseline. Gray means missing data, not zero demand.

2025 is the latest complete calendar year, but country-level coverage is incomplete: Ember has 2025 observations for approximately 91 countries, representing over 90% of global electricity demand. Global totals for 2023–2025 are preliminary and may include estimates. Full-year 2026 figures are not yet comparable because the year is ongoing.

Data and attribution: Ember, Yearly Electricity Data, updated November 3, 2026, CC BY 4.0 license. Global totals are based on the Ember series; the alternative visualization uses the map developed by Our World in Data.
